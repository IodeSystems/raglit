# Scenario: answer a question from the company's documents

**Role.** A support engineer at Larkspur Tools, two months in. Not a developer
of raglit. Somebody told him "the documents are all in raglit".

**Goal, in his words.**

> "A customer asked why checkout broke in March and how long it was down. I
> want the answer, and I want to be able to point at the document it came
> from."

**What he brings.** A browser and the address. He does not know the document's
name or where it is kept.

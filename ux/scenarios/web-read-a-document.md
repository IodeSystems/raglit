# Scenario: read one document closely

**Role.** The office manager at Larkspur Tools. Not technical. Has to check a
term in a contract before a renewal call.

**Goal, in her words.**

> "Somebody said our hosting contract renews by itself. I need to find that
> contract, read what it says about renewal and notice, and know whether what
> I am reading is the whole document."

**What she brings.** A browser and the address. She knows the hosting company
is called Cobalt Cloud.

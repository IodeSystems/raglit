# Lost or stolen company equipment

If a company laptop, phone or security key is missing, act within one hour:

1. Call the Security Desk on extension 4400 (from outside: +1 555 0100 4400).
   They lock the account and start a remote wipe of the device.
2. If it was stolen, report it to the police and get a report number.
3. Open an IT ticket with the report number. IT ships a replacement, usually
   next business day.

Do not wait to see if the device turns up. A wipe can be undone by restoring
from backup; a breach cannot.

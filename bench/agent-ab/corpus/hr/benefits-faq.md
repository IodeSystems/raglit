# Benefits FAQ

**Is there a retirement plan?**
Yes, a 401(k). Thornbury matches 100% of the first 4% of salary you
contribute.

**When is the match mine?**
The employer match vests on a graded schedule: 25% after one year of service,
50% after two years, 75% after three years and 100% after four years. Your
own contributions are always fully yours.

**When does health coverage start?**
On the first day of the month after your start date.

**Is there a learning budget?**
$1,200 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Ask your manager
before you spend it.

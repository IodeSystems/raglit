# Getting reimbursed for business costs

Use this when you paid for something for work with your own money: trips,
customer meals, a conference ticket, driving your own car.

1. Within 30 days of the cost (for a trip: of the day you got back), open
   Brightledger, the finance portal, and choose New claim.
2. Add one line per item. Photograph the itemized receipt for anything over
   $25 and attach it to the line.
3. Choose your team's cost centre. If you do not know it, it is on your team's
   page in the wiki.
4. Submit. Brightledger routes the claim to the right approver for the amount;
   the thresholds are in the Expense Policy.
5. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run.

## Using your own car

Claim mileage, not fuel. The rate is $0.67 per mile, from your normal place of
work (not from home) to the destination and back. Put the start and end
addresses on the claim line.

## Claims older than 30 days

Brightledger will not accept them. Email Finance with the reason; late claims
need the director's sign-off.

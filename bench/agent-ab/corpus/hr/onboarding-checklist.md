# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

Week 1
- Collect laptop from IT; enroll in SSO and the password manager.
- Read the Employee Handbook and the Code of Conduct.
- Meet your onboarding buddy.

Week 2
- Shadow an on-call shift if you are joining an engineering team.
- Complete security training.

Week 4
- First check-in with your manager about goals.

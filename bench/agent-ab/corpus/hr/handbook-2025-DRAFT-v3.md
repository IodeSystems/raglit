# Thornbury Instruments Employee Handbook (2025 edition) — DRAFT v3

DRAFT FOR LEADERSHIP REVIEW. NOT ADOPTED. Do not quote this draft as policy.

## 1. Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's home time zone.

## 2. Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month, up to a balance of 35
days.

## 3. Parental leave

Birth and non-birth parents receive 18 weeks of paid parental leave, to be
taken within the first year after a birth, adoption or foster placement.

## 4. Remote work

Remote work stipend of $75 per month.

## 5. Equipment

Laptops are refreshed every three years.

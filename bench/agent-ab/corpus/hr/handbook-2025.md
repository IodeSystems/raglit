# Thornbury Instruments Employee Handbook (2025 edition)

Effective January 1, 2025. Supersedes the 2024 edition.

## 1. Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's home time zone. Outside core
hours, schedule your own day with your manager's agreement.

## 2. Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month, up to a balance of 30
days. Accrual stops at the cap and resumes when the balance drops below it.
Unused PTO is not paid out except where state law requires it.

## 3. Parental leave

Birth and non-birth parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, to be
taken within the first year after a birth, adoption or foster placement. Leave
may be split into at most three blocks.

## 4. Remote work

Employees who work from home at least three days a week receive a remote work
stipend of $60 per month, paid with the second payroll of each month. See the
People Operations announcement for when the new amount starts.

## 5. Equipment

Laptops are refreshed every three years. Request a refresh through the IT
portal; do not buy hardware on a corporate card.

## 6. Conduct

See the Code of Conduct. Report concerns to People Operations or through the
anonymous line.

# Leaving Thornbury

## Before your last day

- Agree a handover plan with your manager.
- Transfer ownership of documents and repositories you own.

## On your last day

- Return your laptop, badge and security key to IT, in person or with the
  prepaid box IT sends you.
- Your accounts are closed at 18:00 local time.
- People Operations offers an exit interview; it is optional.

## After you leave

Your final paycheck includes accrued, unused PTO where state law requires it.
Benefits end on the last day of the month you leave.

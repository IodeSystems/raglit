# Expense Policy

Owner: Finance. Last reviewed March 2025.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount (single claim) | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to $100 | none (auto-approved) |
| $100.01 to $500 | the employee's manager |
| over $500 | the director of the employee's department |
| over $5,000 | the CFO, after the director |

## City tiers

Per-diem and lodging amounts depend on the tier of the destination city.

- Tier A: New York, San Francisco, Boston, London, Zurich
- Tier B: Denver, Chicago, Seattle, Austin, Toronto
- Tier C: all other destinations

The amounts for each tier are in the Travel Policy.

## Receipts

Attach an itemized receipt to every claim over $25. A card slip alone is not a
receipt.

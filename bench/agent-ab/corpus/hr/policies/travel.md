# Travel Policy

Owner: Finance. Read with the Expense Policy, which defines the city tiers.

## Booking

Book flights through the travel portal at least 14 days ahead. Economy class
for flights under six hours.

## Nightly hotel caps

| Tier | Hotel, per night | Meals per diem |
|---|---|---|
| A | $325 | $95 |
| B | $210 | $75 |
| C | $160 | $60 |

A stay over the cap needs written approval before booking.

## Ground transport

Use public transit or a ride share. Rental cars need a business reason.
